## What it is

Open Diane. Talk. Done.

First tape of the day is morning pages. Anything after is a later tape. Dreams, ideas, mess. Same button.

The voice is not kept. Words are. Full transcript on the phone, like the notebook. A short summary on the card. After a few mornings, a weekly letter that names patterns.

The phone must not cut her off. Screen can dim. Lock screen can close. Diane keeps listening until you tap That’s it.

Bring your own morning. There is no right amount of talking.
