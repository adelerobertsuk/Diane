## 2c. Settings skin pickers

**Canvas for Kate: 1024 × 682 px** (landscape)

That is the gold frame’s exact ratio. Fill the whole canvas edge to edge. No letterboxing in the file. Machine on studio wash `#F3EDE4`. Soft rounded corners optional in the art (app also rounds the frame at 14 pt).

Do not stretch. Keep **1024 × 682**. If you deliver that size, Cursor’s frame fills with no black gaps.

Names: `Diane-pick.png`, `Cooper-pick.png`, `Earle-pick.png`, `Palmer-pick.png`

On phone (approx, full-width card): hero ~ **349 × 232 pt**, grid cell ~ **170 × 113 pt**. Same ratio. Export at **1024 × 682** (or 2× **2048 × 1364** if you want extra crisp).
